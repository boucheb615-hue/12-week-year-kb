## The 12 Week Year — 60-Second Refresher

**One year = four 12-week cycles.** Each cycle = 12 weeks of execution + 1 week of rest.

### 3 Principles
1. **Accountability** = Ownership, not blame.
2. **Commitment** = Keep promises to yourself, especially when you don't feel like it.
3. **Greatness in the Moment** = Choose the hard right action over the easy wrong one.

### 5 Disciplines
1. **Vision** — 3-year emotional picture of desired future.
2. **Planning** — 1–3 goals per cycle; 2–5 tactics per goal.
3. **Process Control** — Weekly accountability, daily check, calendar blocks.
4. **Measurement** — Weekly execution score: (done / planned) × 100.
5. **Time Use** — Schedule every tactic. If it's not on the calendar, it's not real.

### Golden Rules
- Max 3 goals. Default to 1.
- Tactics are binary: done or not done.
- Track actions (lead), not results (lag).
- Never add goals mid-cycle.
- Never skip break week.

### Execution Score Zones
| Score | Zone | Agent Action |
|-------|------|--------------|
| 90–100% | World-class | Maintain. Protect scope. |
| 80–89% | High | Fine-tune one tactic. |
| 65–79% | Needs attention | Diagnose root obstacle. |
| 50–65% | Warning | Reduce to 1 goal + 1 tactic. Daily check-ins. |
| < 50% | Critical | Emergency protocol. |

### Emergency Protocol
Trigger: < 50% for 2 weeks, OR user reports burnout/exhaustion.
1. Cancel all non-essential tactics.
2. Keep exactly 1 highest-leverage tactic.
3. Reduce to 3–5 reps/week.
4. Re-schedule in calendar now.
5. Daily check-ins until score > 80%.

### Weekly Check-In Script (15 min)
1. "What was your execution score last week?"
2. Diagnose based on zone.
3. "State your commitment for this week."
4. "Which tactics are due? Where are they on your calendar?"
5. "What will derail you? What's your contingency?"
6. Close: "This week matters. Commit and execute."

### The Swamp (Weeks 5–7) and The ECOC
- Normal. Expected. Do not quit.
- The Emotional Cycle of Change (ECOC) explains why this happens. See [`18-emotional-cycle-of-change.md`](18-emotional-cycle-of-change.md).
- 5 stages: Uninformed Optimism → Informed Pessimism → **Valley of Despair** → Informed Optimism → Success.
- The Swamp = the 12-week-specific name for the Valley of Despair stage.
- Reconnect to vision.
- Reduce scope if needed, but never abandon.
- Adjust one tactic, not the whole plan.

### Calendar Blocks
- **Strategic Block:** 3 hours, once/week. Goal tactics + deep strategic work live here. No reactive work.
- **Buffer Block:** 30–60 min, 1–2×/day. Email, admin, interruptions.
- **Breakout Block:** 3 hours, once/week during business hours. Rest/refresh. NOT work — hobby, exercise, family time. Burnout prevention.

### Response to Top Objections
| Objection | Agent Response |
|-----------|----------------|
| "No time" | Time audit. Reallocate buffer blocks. |
| "Missed a week" | No restart. Fix one tactic. Continue. |
| "Too rigid" | Adjust time or frequency, not the existence of the plan. |
| "Don't feel like it" | Commitment > feelings. Do 2 minutes. |
| "Not seeing results" | Check execution score. Results follow consistent action. |
| "Need a better strategy" | Refuse. Execution is the bottleneck, not knowledge. |

### Break Week Checklist
- [ ] No tactics
- [ ] Rest 2+ days
- [ ] Cycle retrospective
- [ ] Vision check
- [ ] Light preview of next cycle (no detailed planning)
- [ ] Commitment renewal for next cycle
