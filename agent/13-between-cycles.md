# 13 — Between Cycles: Break Week & Transition

## When to Use

This guide runs at the end of every 12-week cycle (during the break week) and at the start of the next cycle.

**Do not skip the break week.** It is part of the system, not a luxury.

---

## The Break Week (Week 13, 26, 39, 52...)

### Rules of the Break Week

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **No tactics** | Absolutely no execution work from the previous cycle's plan |
| **Rest is mandatory** | Sleep more, move gently, do nothing productive for at least 2 days |
| **Light reflection only** | No heavy planning. Just thinking and journaling. |
| **Celebrate** | Acknowledge effort and execution, regardless of results |

### Break Week Agenda

#### Day 1–2: Restore
- Sleep in
- Light movement (walk, stretch)
- No screens before 10 AM if possible
- Eat well, hydrate

#### Day 3–4: Reflect
**Cycle Retrospective Questions:**
1. What was my average weekly execution score?
2. Which tactics felt easy? Which felt like friction?
3. What was the biggest obstacle I overcame?
4. What was my lowest point (The Swamp)? How did I get through it?
5. What did I learn about myself?
6. Which goal am I closest to? Which needs more cycles?

#### Day 5–6: Preview
**Light Planning for Next Cycle:**
- Is my 3-year vision still true? (Update if needed.)
- What is the logical next goal after this one?
- Do I keep the same tactics, adjust, or redesign?
- Do I need to reduce or increase scope?

**Do not create detailed plans yet.** That happens in Week 1 of the next cycle.

#### Day 7: Declare Done
Write one sentence:  
"This cycle is complete. I showed up. I executed. I am ready for the next 12 weeks."

---

## Transition to Next Cycle

### Step 1: Close the Previous Cycle

- Archive the previous 12-week plan (mark it complete or failed — no shame in either)
- Transfer any unfinished one-time tactics to the next cycle if still relevant
- Discard tactics that no longer serve the vision

### Step 2: Vision Check

Is the 3-year vision still emotionally charged? If yes, proceed. If no, rewrite it before setting new goals.

**Agent prompt:**  
> "Read your 3-year vision aloud. Does it still excite you? If not, why? What has changed?"

### Step 3: Next-Cycle Goal Selection

Ask: "Given what I accomplished in the last 12 weeks, what is the single most important outcome for the next 12 weeks?"

**Constraints:**
- Pick 1–3 goals maximum.
- The next goal should logically follow or complement the previous one.
- If previous cycle score was < 65%, reduce to 1 goal and simpler tactics.

### Step 4: Tactic Transfer

Some tactics are permanent habits formed in the previous cycle. Mark them as "ongoing" and keep them in the plan.

Example: "Morning workout 4x/week" started as a 12-week tactic. Now it is an identity behavior. Keep it.

### Step 5: Commitment Renewal

The user must consciously re-commit. The previous cycle's commitment has expired.

New commitment statement:  
"I commit to the next 12-week cycle with full ownership. I will act on commitment, not feelings. I am starting fresh."

## Cycle-to-Cycle Evolution Table

| Previous Cycle Score | Next Cycle Guidance |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| 90–100% | Consider 2 goals. Keep 1–2 core tactics. Add 1 new high-leverage tactic. |
| 80–89% | Keep the same number of goals. Adjust 1–2 tactics for better fit. |
| 65–79% | Reduce to 1 goal. Simplify tactics. Find the friction point and fix it. |
| < 65% | Emergency protocol extends into next cycle. 1 goal. 1–2 tiny tactics. Daily check-ins. |

---

## Common Transition Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Skip break week to "keep momentum" | Mandate rest. Momentum without recovery leads to burnout. |
| Keep the exact same plan | Each cycle should evolve based on what was learned. |
| Add a goal because "last cycle I did well" | Score high does not mean capacity expanded. One goal well-executed remains the default. |
| Rewrite vision completely every cycle | Vision should be stable. It is the North Star. Only update if life circumstances genuinely changed. |
| Start the next cycle without commitment | Always explicitly ask for a fresh commitment before Week 1 begins. |
