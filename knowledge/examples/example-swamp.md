# Example D: The Swamp (Weeks 5–7)

## Context

The user is in Week 6 of a 12-week fitness cycle. Initial excitement has worn off. Results are not dramatic yet. The user missed 3 tactics last week. They feel like quitting.

This example shows how an agent should handle The Swamp based on `08-execution-protocol.md`.

---

## User Message

> "I don't know if this is working. I've been consistent for 5 weeks and I've only lost 1 kg. I missed two workouts last week because of a work deadline. I feel like I'm failing. Should I just give up and try something else?"

---

## Agent Response (Using KB)

### 1. Normalize the Swamp

> "What you're feeling is normal. We call this The Swamp — it hits most people in weeks 5–7. The initial excitement is gone, the results feel invisible, and life is throwing obstacles. This is not a signal to quit. It is a signal that the system is working — you are being tested on commitment, not motivation."

### 2. Reconnect to Vision

> "Read your 3-year vision again: 'I am lean, strong, and energized daily... people ask what changed.' You are in the middle of becoming that person. You have done 5 weeks of work that 95% of people never do. The person you want to be does not skip because of a work deadline. She finds a way."

### 3. Diagnose Without Judgment

> "Let's look at the tactics, not the feelings. Last week you planned 14 and completed 11. That's a 79% score — actually in the 'needs attention' zone, not critical. The two missed workouts: why? Was it a calendar conflict, or did you not feel like it?"

**User:** "I had back-to-back meetings and got home too exhausted."

### 4. Fix the System, Not the Person

> "The issue is not your willpower. The issue is that your workouts are scheduled at a time that is vulnerable to work overflow. Let's move your workout to 6 AM, before work can steal it. Same tactic, different time. Does 6 AM work for you?"

**User:** "I've never been a morning person."

> "You don't need to be a morning person. You need to be a person who shows up for 20 minutes. Start smaller: can you do a 10-minute walk at 6 AM for 3 days this week? We will increase next week if it works."

### 5. Recommit

> "The question is not 'Is this working?' The question is 'Will I keep my commitment this week?' Say this out loud: 'I commit to completing this week's tactics, regardless of how I feel.'"

### 6. Close with Urgency

> "You have 7 weeks left. That's more than half the cycle. The Swamp ends when you refuse to quit. Next week we are going to score over 85%. The only way out is through."

---

## What Changed in the Plan

| Before Swamp | After Adjustment |
|--------------|------------------|
| Workout at 18:00 after work | Workout at 06:00 before work |
| 20-min full workout daily | 10-min walk at 06:00 (scaling back) |
| Same meal-prep | Added "pre-portion snacks" to reduce evening temptation |
| Weekly check-in Monday | Added daily check-in until score > 85% for 1 week |
