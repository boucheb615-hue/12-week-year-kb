# 09 — Glossary

## A

**Accountability** — Willingness to own actions and results regardless of circumstances. Not consequences; ownership.

**Action Plan** — See "Plan".

**Annualized Thinking** — The mental model that "I have 12 months," which breeds complacency. The 12 Week Year destroys this.

## B

**Binary Completion** — Tactics are scored as done or not done. No partial credit. This removes negotiation.

**Break Week** — One week of rest between 12-week cycles. No tactics. Used for reflection, celebration, and light planning.

**Breakout Block** — 3-hour block scheduled once per week during work hours, exclusively for rest and refresh. Not work — "you time": exercise, hobby, lunch with friends. Prevents burnout.

**Buffer Block** — 30–60 minute calendar blocks (1–2 per day) for reactive work: email, messages, admin.

## C

**Calendar-First Planning** — The rule that tactics must be assigned to specific calendar time slots. If it is not scheduled, it is not real.

**Commitment** — Keeping promises to yourself. Acting on commitment, not feelings.

**Controllable Tactic** — An action fully under the user's control, not dependent on luck or other people's actions.

**Core Concept** — See "Periodization".

## D

**Daily Execution Check** — A 2-minute end-of-day review: Did I do today's tactics? If not, why?

**Disciplines (5)** — Vision, Planning, Process Control, Measurement, Time Use.

## E

**Emergency Protocol** — Triggered when execution score is < 50% for 2 consecutive weeks. Involves radical scope reduction.

**Execution Score** — The percentage of planned tactics completed in a given week. Calculated as (done / planned) × 100.

## F

**Feelings** — Not a valid reason to skip tactics. Commitment operates independently of feelings.

## G
**Goal** — A specific, measurable outcome to be achieved in 12 weeks. Max 1–3 per cycle.

**Greatness in the Moment** — Choosing productive action in the exact moment you are tempted to procrastinate. "Results are not the attainment of greatness, but simply confirmation of it."

## I
**Intentional Imbalance** — The principle that life balance is NOT about equal time in all areas. It's about intentionally choosing what to focus on now. Every 12 weeks, you pick 1–3 areas and make significant progress there, then reassess.

**Interest (vs Commitment)** — Doing something only when circumstances permit. Interest wanes with time or difficulty. The opposite of commitment. "When you're interested, you accept excuses. When you're committed, you accept only results."

## L

**Lag Measure** — An output metric (result of effort). Not tracked in scorekeeping. Examples: revenue, weight, sales.

**Lead Measure** — An input metric (action under user's control). Tracked in scorekeeping. Examples: calls made, words written, workouts done.

## O

**Ownership** — See "Accountability".

## P

**Partial Credit** — Forbidden in 12 Week Year scorekeeping. Binary only.

**Periodization** — From sports training. Breaking the year into short, focused cycles to sustain urgency and focus.

**Plan** — The document translating vision into 12-week goals and weekly/daily tactics.

**Principles (3)** — Accountability, Commitment, Greatness in the Moment.

**Process Control** — Rituals and systems that ensure consistent execution.

## R

**Reactive Work** — Email, messages, unplanned interruptions. Confined to buffer blocks.

**Result** — Lag measure. Outcomes are byproducts of consistent execution.

## S

**Score** — See "Execution Score".

**Scorekeeping** — The discipline of tracking and reviewing weekly execution.

**Strategic Block** — 3-hour uninterrupted block, once per week, for high-leverage strategic work and goal tactics. No reactive work allowed.

**Swamp, The** — The emotional low point of the cycle. Usually occurs in weeks 5–7. Vision and weekly check-ins pull users through it.

## T

**Tactics** — Specific actions the user takes daily or weekly to achieve a 12-week goal.

**Time Use** — The discipline of intentionally designing the calendar to protect priority work.

## V

**Vision** — A vivid, emotional description of the user's desired future (3-year horizon). Used to sustain motivation.

## W

**WAM** — Weekly Accountability Meeting. 15-minute ritual to review score, recommit, and plan the upcoming week.

**Weekly Plan** — The subset of tactics scheduled for the current week, with assigned calendar blocks.
