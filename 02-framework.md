# 02 — Framework: The 3 Principles in Detail

## 1. Accountability (Ownership)

Accountability is not blame, punishment, or consequences. It is the willingness to own your results regardless of circumstances.

**Agent prompt to test this principle:**
> "What is one result you are not happy with right now? How did your actions (or inaction) contribute to it?"

**Pitfalls to flag:**
- Blaming external factors (market, team, weather, mood)
- "I tried" as a substitute for results
- Excusing missed tactics because "things came up"

## 2. Commitment (Promise to Self)

Commitment means doing what you said you would do, especially when it is hard.

**Keys to deep commitment:**
- **Strong desire** — tie the goal to something the user genuinely wants
- **Count the costs** — list obstacles in advance so they are not surprises
- **Act on commitment, not feelings** — "I don't feel like it" is not a valid reason to skip tactics

**Agent prompt:**
> "On a scale of 1–10, how badly do you want this goal? If it is below 7, we need to pick a different goal or reframe this one."

## 3. Greatness in the Moment

Every day presents dozens of micro-decisions. Greatness is choosing the productive action in the moment you are tempted to procrastinate.

**Agent framing:**
- The user does not need to be motivated 24/7.
- They need to execute despite low motivation.
- "Greatness in the moment" is not about heroic effort; it is about not breaking the chain on small daily actions.

## Framework Summary for Agent Context

| Principle | User Belief | Agent Response |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| Accountability | "It's not my fault" | "Let's trace what was in your control." |
| Commitment | "I tried" | "Did you complete the tactic? Binary: yes/no." |
| Greatness in the Moment | "I don't feel like it" | "Commitment is not about feelings. What is the next smallest action?" |
