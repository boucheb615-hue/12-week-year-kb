# Example C: Product Creation (Write a Book)

## Vision (3-year)

"I am a published author with a book that sells consistently on Amazon. I speak at small conferences about the book's topic. Readers email me saying the book changed how they work. I am proud every time I see the cover. The book is a credential that opens doors I didn't know existed."

---

## 12-Week Cycle

**Goal:** Complete the first rough draft of a 30,000-word non-fiction book (≈ 2,500 words/week).

### Tactics

| Tactic | Type | Frequency |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Write 500 words | Recurring | 5x/week (Mon–Fri) |
| Review and outline next chapter | Recurring | 1x/week (Sun) |
| Research 1 topic block | Recurring | 1x/week |
| Send 1 chapter to beta reader | One-time | Weeks 6, 9, 12 |
| Format manuscript in Scrivener/Word | One-time | Weeks 10–12 |

---

## Weekly Goal Example (Week 5)

**Weekly goal:** Write 2,500 words (500×5), complete outline for Chapter 6, and email Chapter 3 beta feedback request.

**Tactics scheduled:**

| Day | Tactic | Time Block |
|-----|--------|------------|
| Mon | Write 500 words | 05:30–07:00 (before work) |
| Tue | Write 500 words | 05:30–07:00 |
| Wed | Write 500 words | 05:30–07:00 |
| Thu | Write 500 words | 05:30–07:00 |
| Fri | Write 500 words | 05:30–07:00 |
| Sat | Off / catch-up if missed | Flexible |
| Sun | Outline Chapter 6 + research | 14:00–16:00 |

**Tactics planned:** 7 (5 writing + 1 outline + 1 research)  
**Tactics done:** 6  
**Execution score:** 86% → High performance

---

## Daily Tactics Summary (Generalized)

| Day | Non-negotiable | Time |
|-----|----------------|------|
| Mon–Fri | Write 500 words | 05:30–07:00 |
| Sun | Outline next week + research | 14:00–16:00 |
| Sat | Buffer / catch-up day | Flexible |

**Obstacles:**
- Morning writing feels impossible → Go to bed by 22:00, coffee ready the night before
- Chapter feels directionless → Outlining on Sunday prevents Monday blank-page terror
- Word count feels abstract → Track in spreadsheet; watch the bar fill up

## Notes on Scope

This example demonstrates that even a project as large as a book becomes manageable when decomposed into tiny, daily, scheduled actions.*The magic is in the daily repetition. 500 × 5 days × 12 weeks = 30,000 words.*
