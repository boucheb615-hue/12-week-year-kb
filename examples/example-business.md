# Example B: Business Growth (Solopreneur)

## Vision (3-year)

"I run a profitable consulting business earning €120k/year while working 4 days/week. I have a waitlist of clients, a reputation for delivering results in 30 days, and I teach my methodology to a small cohort once per quarter. I work from home or anywhere, with full control of my calendar."

---

## 12-Week Cycle

**Goal:** Launch a lead magnet and email sequence that generates 50 qualified leads.

### Tactics

| Tactic | Type | Frequency |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Write 1 LinkedIn post (value + CTA) | Recurring | 3x/week |
| Outreach DM to 5 ideal prospects | Recurring | 3x/week |
| Create lead magnet (PDF guide) | One-time | Weeks 1–3 |
| Build landing page | One-time | Week 3 |
| Write nurture email sequence (5 emails) | One-time | Weeks 2–4 |
| Record 1 short video (YouTube/shorts) | Recurring | 1x/week |
| Review leads and reply within 2 hours | Recurring | Daily (15 min) |
