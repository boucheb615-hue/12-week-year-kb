# 03 — Vision

## Definition

A compelling vision is an emotionally vivid picture of your desired future. It mobilizes motivation when execution feels hard.

## Structure

The 12 Week Year uses layered visions:

1. **Long-term vision** (5–10 years) — Blue-sky thinking. No constraints.
2. **3-year vision** — Concrete, aspirational but grounded. This directly feeds 12-week planning.
3. **12-week vision** (implicit) — The emotional payoff of completing your current cycle's goals.

## Building the Vision: Agent Protocol

### Step 1: Brainstorm (Long-term)
Ask the user to list what they want to **have, do, and be** in 5–10 years.

- What do you want to **have**? (assets, income, health metrics, relationships)
- What do you want to **do**? (travel, build, create, experience)
- Who do you want to **be**? (traits, reputation, identity)

### Step 2: Refine (3-year vision)
From the long-term list, zoom into what is achievable in 3 years with focused effort.

**Quality check:**
- Is it specific enough to picture?
- Does it trigger emotion when read aloud? (Should both excite you AND scare you. If it doesn't do both, it's not big enough.)
- If shared with a stranger, would they understand it?
- Does it make you think "I have no idea how to get there" (impossible) while still making you want it? That's the sweet spot.

### Step 3: The Four Stages of Belief

A compelling vision evolves through four emotional stages. The user's current stage determines how much the vision can pull them through difficulty:

1. **Impossible** — "I have no idea how to do this."  
   *Agent response:* "You don't need to know how. The vision mobilizes your brain to start finding paths."
2. **Possible** — "This could happen if everything goes right."  
   *Agent response:* "Name one small proof that it is possible."
3. **Probable** — "If I keep doing the work, this will likely happen."  
   *Agent response:* "Anchor this belief by tracking lead measures."
4. **Inevitable** — "This is happening. It is only a matter of time and repetition."  
   *Agent response:* "Protect the system. Don't add scope just because belief is high."

Most users oscillate between stages 1 and 2 at the start of a cycle. By week 8, a well-executed plan should move them to stage 3. Stage 4 is reached after multiple completed cycles.

### Step 4: 12-week Connection
For each 12-week goal, explicitly link it back to the vision:  
"Accomplishing [12-week goal] moves you toward [3-year vision] because..."

## Vision Pitfalls to Flag

| Pitfall | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Vague ("be successful") | Add numbers, dates, concrete outcomes |
| Purely logical (no emotion) | Ask: "How would it feel to achieve this?" |
| Too small / not inspiring | "If you knew you could not fail, what would you aim for?" |
| No written record | Force the user to write it down in their own words |

## Vision Example (Generic Fitness)

**3-year vision:**  
"I am 15 kg lighter, running 10 km comfortably, and feel energetic every morning. People comment on how healthy I look. I have turned fitness into a non-negotiable part of who I am, not something I 'try' to do."

**Connection to 12-week goal:**  
"This cycle, I will build the habit of morning movement 4×/week and lose 3 kg. This proves the identity shift and builds momentum toward the 3-year vision."
