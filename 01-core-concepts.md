# 01 — Core Concepts

## Periodization

The foundational breakthrough: **treat 12 weeks as a full year.**

Annualized thinking creates complacency. When the deadline is 12 months away, the brain perceives infinite time. The result: 30–40% of productivity happens in the last 60 days (the "year-end push"). Periodization compresses the timeline to force consistent urgency throughout.

**Why 12 weeks?**
- Short enough to feel urgent
- Long enough to produce meaningful results
- Predictable (assumptions degrade less than over 12 months)
- Fits neatly into 4 cycles per calendar year + 1 week breaks

## The 12 Week Year Cycle Structure

```
Calendar Year = 4 x (12-week cycle + 1-week break)

Cycle 1: Weeks 1–12 → Break week 13
Cycle 2: Weeks 14–25 → Break week 26
Cycle 3: Weeks 27–38 → Break week 39
Cycle 4: Weeks 40–51 → Break (or holiday)
```

**Team application:** The system scales to teams. Each team member has their own 12-week plan, and the team holds a collective Weekly Accountability Meeting (WAM). The principles and disciplines are identical; the difference is public commitment to peers instead of private commitment to self. This KB focuses on individual coaching; team add-ons require only slight modifications to `05-process-control.md` (group WAM agenda) and `08-execution-protocol.md` (peer visibility).

## Three Principles (Mindset)

1. **Accountability** — Ownership of actions and results regardless of circumstances. It is not consequences; it is ownership.
2. **Commitment** — Keeping promises to yourself. Do what you said you would do, especially when you do not feel like it.
3. **Greatness in the Moment** — Choosing the hard right action over the easy wrong one, in real time, every day.

## Five Disciplines (Action)

1. **Vision** — A compelling, emotional picture of your desired future.
2. **Planning** — Translating vision into 12-week goals and weekly tactics.
3. **Process Control** — Systems and rituals that keep execution on track.
4. **Measurement (Scorekeeping)** — Weekly execution scoring to drive accountability.
5. **Time Use** — Intentional calendar design to protect priority work.

## The Performance Equation

```
Results = Knowledge × Execution
```

Most people over-index on knowledge (new strategies, books, courses) and under-index on execution. The 12 Week Year is an execution system. It assumes the user already knows what to do; the system forces the doing.

## Key Reminders for Agent

- Do not let the user set more than 3 goals per 12-week cycle.
- Default to 1 goal if the user is new or overwhelmed.
- Emphasize execution score over outcome score.
- Break weeks are for rest, reflection, and light planning — not work.
- The user will feel emotionally low mid-cycle (weeks 5–7). The vision exists to pull them through this.
