# 19 — Competitive Landscape: Achieve App (Official Competitor)

## Overview

**Achieve** is the official 12 Week Year application owned and operated by **The Execution Company** — the company founded by Brian P. Moran and Michael Lennington, authors of *The 12 Week Year*. It is the canonical software implementation of the methodology.

This file serves as competitive intelligence for the agent you're building. Understanding what the official app does (and doesn't do) defines your differentiation strategy.

## Achieve App — Key Capabilities

### 1. Vision & Goal Setting
- Define a personal vision (the "WHY")
- Set 12-week goals tied to that vision
- Write tactics (specific actions) to execute toward goals

### 2. Weekly Execution Tracking
- Scorekeeping on a weekly basis (execution score = tactics done / tactics planned)
- Track progress against 12-week targets
- "Measure your tactics and score your performance to stay on track"

### 3. AI Coaching (Beta)
- Quote from the page: *"The Achieve AI has been created from the ground up off the 12 Week Year system to assist you just like one of our coaches would. Write tactics, set goals, and get feedback from an expert who is there 24/7."*
- Some AI features marked as beta.

### 4. Templates
- Pre-built 12-week plan templates to accelerate onboarding
- Domain-specific goal and tactic templates

### 5. Calendar Integration (Premium tier)
- Schedule tactics into calendar
- Premium tier feature

### 6. Training & Peer Coaching (Elite tier)
- Access to 12 Week Year training courses
- Live peer-to-peer mentorship and coaching sessions
- Includes access to Brian Moran's coaching methodology

## The System — Official Training Program

The official training defines **3 main phases**:

1. **Craft a Vision** — "Vision is the starting point of high performance and a life of significance. A powerful vision defines your WHY."
2. **Set 12 Week Goals** — "Set goals that move towards your vision. In 12 weeks you will be amazed at what you can accomplish."
3. **Execute Weekly** — "Identify and execute the tactics needed to accomplish your goal in 12 weeks. Track and score your progress."

This 3-phase structure is built on the **5 Disciplines**:
1. Vision
2. Planning
3. Process Control
4. Scorekeeping
5. Time Use

## Competitive Analysis — Weaknesses & Gaps

| Weakness | Detail | Your Opportunity |
|----------|--------|------------------|
| **AI is in beta, behind paywall** | No free AI coaching tier. AI features still experimental. | Your agent can offer AI coaching as the CORE product, not a premium add-on. |
| **Rigid structure** | Follows the official methodology strictly — no room for adjacent productivity systems. | Your agent can bridge 12 Week Year with other frameworks (GTD, Deep Work, Atomic Habits). |
| **App-first, not conversational** | Achieve is a traditional app with AI bolted on. The experience is form-based, not conversational. | Your agent is conversation-first: daily chat check-ins feel like a human coach, not a tracking app. |
| **No proactive accountability** | Achieve tracks input — it doesn't aggressively chase you when you miss tactics. | Your agent can be proactive: "You missed 2 tactics. What happened? Let's fix this NOW." |
| **No emotional intelligence** | AI coaching is generic. It doesn't map to ECOC stages or The Swamp like a trained coach would. | Your agent has `16-emotional-cycle.md` and `18-emotional-cycle-of-change.md` embedded — it recognizes emotional zones. |
| **No deep vision crafting** | Achieve has a vision field, but no guided vision protocol (Four Stages of Belief, excited & scared test). | Your agent has `03-vision.md` — a full guided protocol. |
| **Closed ecosystem** | Achieve is a standalone app. No API, no integrations beyond calendar (premium). | Your agent can integrate anywhere (Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, voice, API). |
| **No multi-language** | English-only. | Your agent can coach in any language. |
