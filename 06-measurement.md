# 06 — Measurement (Scorekeeping)

## Philosophy

The 12 Week Year scorekeeping is a **lagging indicator** for execution, not results.

It answers: "Did I do what I said I would do?" — not "Did I get the outcome I wanted?"

This is a crucial distinction. You cannot always control outcomes. You can always control execution.

## The Weekly Scorecard

### Step 1: List Weekly Tactics
Each goal has tactics assigned for the week. These are binary: **done** or **not done**.

### Step 2: Calculate Execution Score

```
Execution Score = (Number of tactics completed / Number of tactics planned) × 100
```

**Example:**
- 4 tactics planned for the week
- 3 completed, 1 missed
- Execution score = (3 / 4) × 100 = 75%

### Step 3: Track Over Time

| Week | Tactics Planned | Tactics Done | Score |
|------|-----------------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| 2 | 5 | 4 | 80% |
| 3 | 5 | 3 | 60% |

## The Meaning of Scores

| Score Range | Interpretation | Agent Action |
|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| 90–100% | World-class execution | Maintain; do not add scope |
| 80–89% | High performance | Fine-tune one tactic |
| **65–79%** | Needs attention | Diagnose root obstacle |
| **50–65%** | Warning | Reduce scope to 1 goal + 1 tactic; switch to daily check-ins |
| **< 50%** | Critical | Emergency protocol: drop to survival mode |

## Monthly / 6-Week / 12-Week Score

At the end of 12 weeks, calculate the **cycle execution score**:

```
Cycle Score = Average of weekly scores
```

Target: **85%+** for meaningful results.

## Lag vs Lead Measures

| Type | Definition | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| Lead measure | Inputs you control | "Wrote for 30 min daily" |
| Lag measure | Outputs you hope for | "Finished novel" |

**12 Week Year scorekeeping tracks lead measures exclusively.** The outcome is a byproduct of execution.

## Measurement Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Tracking results instead of tactics | Redirect to weekly execution score |
| Complex scoring (partial credit) | Binary only — done or not done |
| No weekly review | Mandate Monday score review |
| Too many metrics | 1 metric per goal: execution % |

## Agent Scorekeeping Prompt

At the end of each week, present:
> "This week you planned X tactics and completed Y. Your execution score is Z%. Here's what that means for your trajectory..."

Then label the zone (world-class / high / needs attention / critical) and prescribe the matching action.
